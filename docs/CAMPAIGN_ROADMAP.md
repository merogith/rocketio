# Campaign ideas

Sentinel Training ships as two complete operations: Cold Spool and Bunkerline. Completing Bunkerline ends training and returns to custom skirmishes; no additional operation is required to finish the arc.

These names are retained as optional future design ideas, not available missions or promised releases: No-Sky, Overmatch, Dagger, Breach, Keystone, Empty Belt, Bait and Last Regime.
